Why Small Businesses Still Need a Fast Website

Published on Ravi Digital • Practical guide

A social profile is useful, but a website gives a business a place it controls for services, pricing, contact details, trust signals and search visibility.

Make the first screen clear

A visitor should quickly understand what the business offers and what action to take next.

Design for mobile

Business sites should be comfortable to browse on a phone, with readable text, fast-loading images and easy contact actions.

Build for action

Useful calls to action include booking, calling, messaging, requesting a quote or viewing a menu.
